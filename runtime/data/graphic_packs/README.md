# Graphic packs

Files from Cemu's community graphic packs, https://github.com/cemu-project/cemu_graphic_packs
(commit 975f3759d7837da5a374150347d3202360b13ca3, `src/BreathOfTheWild/...`), released under CC0 1.0.
Only the Metal (`_msl`) shaders and `rules.txt` are kept. They are compiled into the runtime
(`graphic_packs_data.cpp`, generated by CMake) and chosen in Settings > Graphics > Enhancements.

- `Enhancements/`: Clarity colour presets, depth of field, reflections and reflection range.
  Made by Kiri, Jamie, M&&M, Pig, Skyth and the Clarity preset creators.

Changed here: `Enhancements/cb0e6e8cbec4502a_..._ps_msl.txt`, depth of field off sampled with a float4
coordinate and did not compile; it samples the centre the depth-of-field path uses.
